## Narrow policy increment

Feature `sunfish-ruleset-service-handoff` inherits the full earlier coverage profile. It is explicitly enabled by the native runtime. Merely compiling the feature leaves the Owner policy disabled. The Owner records the first accepted exact ruleset RRQ's opaque handle and peer through its existing validated request-event path. This policy state is separate from debug observations and optional clocks.

One additional ordinary service step is allowed only after the original first EFS missing-file ERROR has actually passed normal transmit admission (the existing real EFS one-shot latch), if the exact first ruleset remains the normal oldest pending TFTP handle. It refuses an already spent opportunity, a known matching close, any retained TFTP reply, or pending MCFG/firmware close cleanup. It does not select around an older request or bypass normal service, provider routing, receive-buffer return, generation/status validation, queue capacity, same-peer flow or QRTR credits. It spends before fallible work; errors retain normal terminal containment. The ordinary Runtime services still continue after this bounded call.

At the exact first ruleset's normal admission of the immutable19-byte ERROR1, a separate one-shot writer opportunity runs only if EFS admission has occurred and no matching close is known. It permits at most four ordinary Adapter publication calls, repeating the established checked EFS writer pattern. Each call uses normal `publish_next` and its fresh FIFO checks and selection. RX_DONE, advertisement and grant ACK may consume the group; the first other committed operation ends it. This does not force the target packet, skip QRTR credits or invert advertisement-before-ACK. Pending RX, backpressure, awaiting intent or idle stop the group. The real blocked-write/read-notification fallback is retained. Uncertain writes stop the Owner; they do not synthesize publication, completion, credit or buffer release.

If normal admission is FlowBusy/NoCapacity, the service opportunity is spent and the exact retained reply remains under existing checks. Its separate writer opportunity can run once after later real admission, unless a matching close has become known. The change does not permit overlapping ruleset replies, cancel a published predecessor, or retire a flow on close. No optional clock site, new shared mapping, capability or file data is introduced. Additional ordinary checked service/publication calls do add real work and can change timing. New policy state is initialized in both Owner construction paths; terminal generation handling remains inherited.
